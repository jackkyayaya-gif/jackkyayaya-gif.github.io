musordrop — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-04

musordrop is a personal tool that uploads the user’s own short videos to the user’s own TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API.

Data collected

TikTok OAuth access and refresh tokens only. They are stored locally on the user’s own computer (in a local .env file) and are used solely to identify the connected account and to upload/post videos to that same account.

Data sharing

None. Tokens are never sold, shared, or sent to any third party. The tool performs no analytics or tracking and collects no data about other people.

Retention and revocation

Tokens remain on the user’s machine until deleted. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok settings (Security & login → Apps and sessions).
